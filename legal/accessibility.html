Accessibility statement

Last updated 12 November 2026 · version 3

This is a plain-language statement written by the project's author. It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

We want everyone to be able to use the app. It aims at the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA. It has been checked by automated tests and by hand in a browser, not yet with a real screen reader on a phone, so we say it partly meets that standard and list below what is missing.

What works

  • Keyboard. Everything in the app can be reached and operated without a mouse or touch: buttons, the legend of lines, notices, Settings and panels. Panels take focus when they open, keep Tab inside while they are open, and give focus back when they close. Escape closes the newest panel.
  • The map's stations by keyboard. Focus the map (Tab), then press N and P to step through the stations in view (nearest the centre first); the station is ringed on the map and announced. Enter opens it, L lists them all, Esc clears. A "Browse the stations in view" button does the same for screen readers. Arrow keys pan the map and + and - zoom it.
  • Screen readers. Buttons, switches (with on and off), dialogs and the map have names, in English, Turkish, Arabic and French. Station choices and results are announced.
  • Colour and contrast. Text colour against its background was measured in every style (Neon, Atlas, Calm, Paper) in the dark and light themes, and with high contrast on, and meets 4.5:1 (WCAG AA). Line chips choose black or white text to stay readable on any line colour.
  • Motion. The system "reduce motion" setting and Settings, Accessibility, Reduce motion both calm the animations.
  • Text size. Settings, Text size makes text larger. Languages that read right to left (Arabic) are laid out right to left.

What does not work yet, or works differently

  • Pinch-zooming the page is switched off on purpose. The app should behave like an app: only the map zooms and pans, and pinching a tab or a panel no longer zooms and distorts the whole page. This is a trade-off against the guideline that text should be resizable. To enlarge text use Settings, Text size, or your device's own magnifier (Android: Accessibility, Magnification, or "Force enable zoom"; iPhone: Accessibility, Zoom, or Display and Text Size).
  • No real screen reader testing yet. TalkBack, VoiceOver and NVDA have not been used on the app. Expect rough edges and tell us about them.
  • The names drawn on the map (streets, districts, places) are part of a map picture and are not read by screen readers; stations can be reached with the keys above, and places with the search box.
  • The moving carriages are a visual simulation with no equivalent for a screen reader; timetables and departures are available as text.
  • Legal pages are available in English and Turkish only.
  • Colours of lines on the map against the map have not been measured against the 3:1 guideline for graphics.

Tell us about a barrier

If something does not work for you, write to mohamedyounis248@gmail.com and say what you were trying to do, what device and assistive technology you use, and what happened. We read every message and fix what we can; this is a personal project, so we cannot promise a date.
