Cookies and stored data

Last updated 3 November 2026 · version 1

This is a plain-language statement written by the project's author. It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

Cookies

The app sets no cookies, and uses no cookie-like tracking (no tracking IDs, no fingerprinting, no advertising or analytics scripts). It also does not use IndexedDB or session storage.

What it stores instead

The app saves a small amount of data in your browser's own local storage, on your device only. It is never sent to the author or anyone else. Each item is created only when the thing it remembers happens.

Offline copies

So the app opens without a connection, a service worker keeps copies of the app's own files in your browser's cache storage, in four caches whose names begin with raynet-: the app page (raynet-shell-…), its scripts, typefaces, data files and icons (raynet-static-v1), the street-map tiles you have viewed (raynet-vtiles-v1, up to 3,000 files) and the satellite or backup map tiles you have viewed (raynet-tiles-v2, up to 3,000 files). Save this area adds the tiles for one screenful on purpose; nothing is downloaded in bulk.

Why there is no cookie banner

Everything above is stored so the features you use can work (your settings, your saved places, offline use), is created because of something you did, and never leaves your device. In our reading that does not call for consent. The one real choice the app offers, whether to allow online look-ups, is asked about once in a notice with two equal buttons, and explained in the privacy notice.

How to delete it

  • All of it, in one tap: Settings, Search and privacy, Delete all my data on this device. It removes every item above, the offline copies and the service worker, then restarts the app like a first visit.
  • Just trips and favourites: Clear saved trips and favourites, in the same place.
  • Your browser: clearing this site's data in your browser's settings removes everything too.

Other projects published under the same github.io address keep their own data; the button removes only what this app wrote (keys starting irn_ and caches starting raynet-).
