Legal and privacy

Last updated 3 November 2026 · version 1

This is a plain-language statement written by the project's author. It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

İstanbul Ray-Net is a free, non-commercial, open-source map of public transport in Turkish cities. These pages say what it does with information, what you can expect from it, and where its data and software come from.

  • Privacy notice: what the app keeps, who it talks to, and your choices.
  • Terms of use: what the app is, what it is not, that it is free (no fees, no refunds needed), and its limits.
  • Cookies and stored data: exactly what is saved on your device, and how to delete it.
  • Credits and licences: whose data, software, typefaces and images the app uses.
