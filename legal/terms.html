1. What this is

İstanbul Ray-Net is a free map and journey planner for public transport in Turkish cities (metro, tram, ferry, bus and more). It is made by the author of İstanbul Ray-Net, an individual (GitHub: Hero4mohamed) as a non-commercial, open-source project. It is not an official service of Metro İstanbul, İETT, İBB, Şehir Hatları, TCDD or any other operator or authority, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

2. Check before you travel

  • Timetables, fares, step-free information and notices come from official and open sources and may be out of date, incomplete or wrong. Fares and walking times are estimates.
  • The carriages you see moving on the map are a simulation worked out from published timetables and frequencies. They are not vehicle positions, and the "next departures" board says MODELLED when it is derived that way.
  • Rail and ferry notices show how old they are; if they have not been refreshed for a while the app says so.
  • Always confirm with the operator's own channels before a journey that matters.

Nothing in the app is for sale. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase, no auto-renewal and no advertising. The only cost to you is the mobile data your own phone plan charges for map tiles and data (the "Save this area" button downloads roughly 2 MB for one screenful of map).
