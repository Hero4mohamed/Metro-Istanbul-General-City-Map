## 1. Disruption-aware planning

**Why:** the map draws a closed stretch while the planner can still send people through it (`suspendedRefs()` in `07-routing.js` blocks only lines suspended end to end).

**Each stretch of a notice gets one of four kinds:**

| Kind | From | Planner | Shown |
|---|---|---|---|
| `closed` | a `segment` notice with severity `major`, or Turkish wording that says trains do not run between A and B | avoided | a line at the top of the results: "Avoiding M7 A–B · notice checked … ago", tap for the operator's text |
| `shuttle` | "A-B arasında (ise) mekik" | routable | an amber chip on that leg, with the operator's text |
| `unmentioned` | the part of the line outside both the "normal" and the "shuttle" stretches | routable | **a red warning** on that leg: "Not listed as running in the operator's notice", with the operator's text |
| whole line | `line` + `major` (today's rule) | avoided | the same top line |

- **Safety valve:** when the rail notices are older than the existing stale limit (`ANN_STALE_MIN`, 8 h), nothing is avoided. Everything becomes a warning that says the notices may be out of date.
  - My call: the valve applies to whole-line suspensions too, so one rule covers everything. Today a whole-line suspension is avoided whatever its age.
- **The map is unchanged:** the V4.8.1 caution band still covers every affected stretch.
- **Implementation outline:**
  - `noticeSections()` returns kinds (it keeps returning the merged stretches for the map).
  - Blocking works per station node of that line: inside a closed stretch you cannot board, alight or ride through. The stretch's end stations stay usable if they are served on the other side.

## 3. The Environment tab

**Why:** the owner wants real topography "as if it were Google Earth" and 3D buildings, in a new tab where they can be adjusted.

- **A separate 3D view on MapLibre 4.7.1** (already shipped, BSD). The main Leaflet map is not rewritten.
- **Opening the tab:**
  - it opens at the main map's centre and zoom, tilted, with the map's turn as its bearing;
  - the main map's MapLibre basemap is taken off while the tab is open (one heavy map at a time);
  - leaving calls `map.remove()` and puts the basemap back.
- **Terrain:**
  - AWS Terrain Tiles `elevation-tiles-prod`, Terrarium PNG (keyless, CORS `*`, checked 19 Nov). Not the `-eu` mirror (403).
  - Hill shading plus 3D terrain.
- **Buildings:**
  - our OpenFreeMap tiles' `building` layer, extruded with `render_height` / `render_min_height`;
  - the filter `hide_3d != true`;
  - buildings at exactly 5 m (OpenMapTiles' default) drawn muted;
  - a tap readout: "~N m · OpenStreetMap height or floor estimate", or "Height probably not mapped (5 m default)".
- **Lines:** the city's live lines (and the planned route, if any) drawn over the terrain, in their colours. Station dots too.
- **Sun lighting:**
  - the sun's direction and height computed on the device from the view's centre and the time (with a time-of-day slider);
  - it drives the hill shading's light direction and the 3D light;
  - called "Sun lighting", never "shadows" (MapLibre 4.7.1 casts none).
- **Relief:** 1× (true) or 1.5×, badged "Exaggerated 1.5×".
- **Presets:**
  - Light (hill shading on the flat map);
  - Standard (3D terrain);
  - Full (terrain + buildings).
- **Default preset:** Standard until it is measured on the Galaxy A02. A small performance readout (load time, frames per second) in the tab lets the owner measure it on the phone, without cables.
- **Offline:** terrain needs the internet. Without it, the tab shows the flat map with "3D terrain needs an internet connection". The offline packs are not changed.
- **Credit:** the terrain tile maker's full required credit list (Mapzen; Copernicus EU-DEM; NOAA ETOPO1; USGS 3DEP/GMTED2010/SRTM; and the national ones listed on that page), shown in the tab's info, the credits page (EN/TR) and `DATA-SOURCES.md`. Exact wording copied from `tilezen/joerd/docs/attribution.md`.
- **Privacy:** the tab fetches terrain tiles from AWS (Amazon), which sees the device's address and the area viewed, like the basemap provider. The privacy notice's list of services contacted gains this line (EN/TR). Nothing else is sent.
